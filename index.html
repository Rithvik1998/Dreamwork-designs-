About Us

At the heart of every home lies furniture that tells a story—your story. Since 2019, we have been dedicated to crafting custom furniture pieces that reflect the unique vision and lifestyle of each client we serve.

Founded by Satvik Srivastav in 2019, our company was born from a passion for creating exceptional, personalized furniture that goes beyond the ordinary. Located in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, our state-of-the-art factory combines traditional woodworking techniques with modern precision tools.

We are committed to responsible business practices and environmental stewardship. All our materials are ethically sourced, ensuring that our beautiful furniture comes with the peace of mind that it was created sustainably and responsibly.
